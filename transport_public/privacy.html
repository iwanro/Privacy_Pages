The HaiBus application is provided by Iulian Ivan; the transport services and data displayed do not belong to him and are not under his control — they belong to the operators mentioned below.

1. What we never collect

  • Name, email address, phone number: we never ask for any of them.
  • User account: there is none, you cannot sign in.
  • Your location: the application does not request or record your position. The map shows vehicles in the network, not you.
  • Contacts, messages, photos, files: we do not request access to them.
  • Search history: stays on your device, in the local database.
  • Advertising data: there are no ads in the application and no advertising SDK.

We do not sell data and do not use it for advertising. We make no automated decisions about you and perform no profiling.

3. Data that stays on your phone

  • The network timetable, downloaded once and updated when it changes.
  • Stops and routes you have marked as favorites.
  • Application settings, including acceptance of this document at first launch.

These are deleted when you uninstall the application or clear its data in Android settings. We hold no copy, because there is no account.

4. Who helps us operate the service

The providers below process data on our behalf, for the functions described above:

  • Google Ireland Limited — Firebase Cloud Messaging, Firebase Crashlytics, Google Play Billing.
  • Adapty — subscription management.
  • Cloudflare, Inc. — hosting of the API server.

5. Transfers outside the European Economic Area

The providers above are companies also established in the United States of America. Transfers rely on the adequacy decision for the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified, and otherwise on the Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission. You can request details about the safeguards applied at the contact address above.

6. How long we keep the data

  • Notification token: as long as the application is installed. It is removed when you disable notifications or uninstall the application; the server deletes it at the next check of inactive topics.
  • Error reports: up to 90 days, then automatically deleted.
  • Subscription data: as long as required by tax and accounting obligations under the law.
  • Technical server logs: 30 days.

7. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, and portability under the GDPR. Write to the contact address above and we will reply within one month.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the National Supervisory Authority for Personal Data Processing (ANSPDCP) in Romania.

For data within Google applications you may also use the controls in your Google account.

8. Children

The application is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data about them. Subscriptions are intended for adults.

9. Security

All communication with the server uses HTTPS. We store no passwords and keep no user databases. No method is infallible, but the exposed surface is small by design: we have no accounts and no personal data beyond the table above.

10. Changes

When this policy changes significantly, we update the date above and, for important changes, show a notice in the application before they take effect.
