Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 22, 2026 · Last updated: October 22, 2026 · Version 1.0

This Privacy Policy explains how the Jellyfall mobile game for Android ("Jellyfall", "the Game", "we", "us") handles personal data. It applies to the Game distributed through Google Play under the application ID ro.iwan.jellyfall.

Short version. Jellyfall has no accounts, no login, and no servers of its own. Your scores and settings stay on your device. The only outside service is an advertising SDK (CAS.AI) that shows optional ads and processes device and advertising data for that purpose. You can accept or decline personalized advertising in the consent form, and change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is Iwan, an individual developer established in Romania, European Union.

  • Email: iwanro@users.noreply.github.com
  • Contact form: github.com/iwanro/jellyfall_android/issues

We are established in the European Union, so we are not required to appoint an EU representative under Article 27 GDPR. We have not appointed a Data Protection Officer: the processing is not large-scale, systematic or special-category.

2. Scope: what this policy covers

This policy covers the Android app Jellyfall. It does not cover Google Play itself, the Android operating system, or websites and stores you reach through links. Those services have their own privacy policies.

3. What data is processed, and why

3.1 Data that stays on your device

The Game stores the following locally in Android app storage (SharedPreferences). It is not transmitted to us, because we operate no backend:

  • your best score, current settings (sound, music, vibration, on-screen buttons, language);
  • whether you have seen the first-run tutorial;
  • local counters used to adapt difficulty and to avoid showing ads too often, including the number of app opens and the first/last open day;
  • your advertising consent choice, so we do not ask again on every launch.

Deleting the app or clearing its storage deletes this data. We cannot recover it, and we cannot read it.

3.2 Data processed by the advertising SDK

Jellyfall displays optional rewarded ads (continue after a game over, double your score) and occasional interstitial ads between runs. Ads are served through CAS.AI (Clever Ads Solutions, operated by CLEAR INVEST LTD), a mediation platform that may route ad requests to partner ad networks. When ads are requested, the SDK and its partners may process:

CategoryExamplesWhy
Advertising and device identifiers Google Advertising ID (resettable), App Set ID Ad delivery, frequency capping, measurement, fraud prevention
Network and technical data IP address, connection type, mobile carrier, approximate location derived from IP (country, city) Ad delivery, geo-targeting, fraud prevention
Device and app data Device model, manufacturer, operating system version, screen size, app version, language, time zone Ad compatibility, rendering, measurement
Ad interaction data Impressions, clicks, video completion, reward events, error and performance data Measurement, billing to advertisers, fraud prevention
Consent data Your advertising consent signals (personalized or non-personalized ads) Compliance with privacy law and with the ad platform's requirements

We do not receive your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera data, and the Game requests none of those permissions. We do not collect precise GPS location.

Permissions. The Game itself declares only INTERNET and VIBRATE. The advertising SDKs bundled through the mediation platform add their own permissions, which you can see before installing on the Play Store listing:

  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, ACCESS_WIFI_STATE — to check whether a connection is available and whether you are on Wi-Fi;
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID and the Android Privacy Sandbox permissions ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS — to read the advertising ID and serve and measure ads;
  • com.google.android.finsky.permission.BIND_GET_INSTALL_REFERRER_SERVICE — install attribution by the store;
  • WAKE_LOCK, FOREGROUND_SERVICE — to keep an ad download or a full-screen ad from being interrupted;
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — declared by one of the ad SDKs; the Game never asks you for it and never posts notifications.

None of these give access to your contacts, calendar, photos, files, microphone, camera or precise location, and none of them are requested by the Game's own code.

3.3 What we never do

  • We do not create user accounts or profiles tied to your identity.
  • We do not sell personal data for money.
  • We do not use your data for automated decisions that produce legal effects for you.
  • We do not ask for or process special-category data (health, biometrics, political opinions, etc.).

5. Your advertising choices

On first launch, users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are shown a consent form provided by CAS.AI. The form lets you accept or refuse personalized advertising and lists the advertising partners involved. If you refuse, the Game can still show non-personalized ads, and the Game remains fully playable either way.

You can reopen the form at any time in the Game through Settings → Privacy options, which reopens the CAS.AI consent management platform. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in the Android system settings under Settings → Privacy → Ads (path may vary by device).

CAS.AI's own practices are described in the Clever Ads Solutions privacy policy: cas.ai/privacy-policy. The mediation adapters included in this version of the Game may include Google (AdMob demand), Meta Audience Network, AppLovin, Unity Ads, ironSource, Mintegral, InMobi, Vungle (Liftoff) and Pangle (ByteDance). That list depends on the adapter set built into the app version you are using and can change with updates; CAS.AI publishes the current partner list in its documentation. Each partner network maintains its own privacy policy.

6. Sharing and international transfers

We do not share data ourselves, because we hold none. When the Game requests an ad, the request goes from your device to CAS.AI and, through mediation, to the ad network that wins the auction. CAS.AI and its partners act as independent controllers or processors for their own advertising purposes.

These companies are mainly located in the United States and other countries outside the EEA. Transfers rely on the safeguards those companies have put in place, such as Standard Contractual Clauses, the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where the recipient is certified, and additional technical measures. We do not operate our own servers, so no data of yours is stored by us at all.

7. Retention

  • On your device: settings, score and consent choice are kept until you delete the app or clear its storage.
  • Advertising data: retained by CAS.AI and its partners under their own retention rules, described in their privacy policies. We cannot access or delete that data directly.
  • Messages you send us: kept only as long as needed to answer the request and to comply with legal obligations.

8. Your rights

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, you have the right to:

  • access the personal data processed about you, and obtain a copy;
  • rectify inaccurate data;
  • erase data (right to be forgotten);
  • restrict processing;
  • data portability;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests, including profiling for direct marketing;
  • withdraw consent at any time;
  • not be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing that produces legal or similarly significant effects;
  • lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

To exercise a right, write to iwanro@users.noreply.github.com. We answer within one month, and may ask for information needed to identify the data you mean. Because we hold no account data, in most cases the practical answer is that we hold nothing about you, and we will confirm that in writing.

The supervisory authority for Romania is the National Supervisory Authority for Personal Data Processing (ANSPDCP), dataprotection.ro. In the UK it is the Information Commissioner's Office, ico.org.uk. You may complain to the authority in the country where you live, work or where the alleged breach happened.

9. United States: California and other states

If you live in California, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, Virginia or another US state with a comprehensive privacy law, you have rights that may include: to know what personal information is collected, to delete it, to correct it, to opt out of its sale or sharing for cross-context behavioral advertising, and to opt out of certain profiling. You also have the right not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

  • Categories collected: identifiers (advertising ID, IP address), internet or network activity (ad interactions), and inferences drawn for advertising purposes. Approximate location derived from IP may count as geolocation under some statutes.
  • Sale or sharing: we do not exchange personal information for money. Personalized advertising may be treated as "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising under the CPRA. You can opt out by choosing non-personalized ads in the consent form, or in Settings → Privacy options.
  • How to exercise: use the consent form, or email iwanro@users.noreply.github.com. We may need to verify your request, but we hold no identifying records, so most requests result in a confirmation that we hold no personal information about you.
  • Authorized agents may submit requests with written permission from the consumer.

We do not knowingly collect, sell or share the personal information of consumers under 16 years of age.

10. Children

Jellyfall is not directed to children. The Game does not target children under 13 (under 16 in the EEA, or the applicable age of digital consent in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. The advertising shown is not configured as child-directed advertising.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has used the Game in a way that provided personal data, contact us at iwanro@users.noreply.github.com and we will help you delete the local data (delete the app or clear its storage) and, where relevant, request deletion from the advertising provider.

11. Security

The Game itself stores a handful of preferences on your device, protected by the Android app sandbox. It has no backend, no database and no user credentials, so there is no server of ours that could be breached. Advertising traffic between the Game and CAS.AI is transmitted over encrypted connections (TLS/HTTPS). No system is perfectly secure, and no method of transmission is guaranteed to be immune to interception, but the amount of data at stake here is limited to advertising identifiers.

12. Cookies and local storage

The Game is a native Android app and sets no browser cookies. It uses Android local storage (SharedPreferences) for the settings, score and consent choice described above. The advertising SDK uses local storage for its own technical operation (caching, frequency control, consent state).

13. Third-party links and ads

Ads and store pages can open external sites or install pages managed by third parties. We do not control their content or their data practices. Read their privacy policies before providing data. If you see an ad you consider deceptive or inappropriate, report it to us at the contact address above, with a screenshot and the approximate time, and we will forward it to the advertising provider.

Material changes will be announced in the Game or in the store listing. If a change requires your consent, we will ask for it before the change takes effect.
