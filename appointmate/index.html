AppointMate Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

AppointMate ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what the app stores locally, what is sent to the cloud to deliver push reminders, and the controls you have.

1. Information We Collect

Stored on your device

  • Appointment data: client names, phone numbers, dates/times, duration, descriptions and addresses
  • Settings: theme, SMS templates, business details used on estimates and invoices
  • Progress notes and images: the notes and photos you add
  • Documents: estimates, invoices, payments and warranties

Sent to the cloud for push reminders

To deliver push notifications while the app is closed, the minimum fields needed are copied to Firebase Firestore:

  • client name, phone number, appointment time, description and category
  • a device identifier and the Firebase Cloud Messaging token

The copy is deleted when you delete the appointment, the client, or all data.

Usage counters

A pseudonymous device hash and monthly appointment/image counters are stored on Cloudflare. They contain no client data and are used only to enforce the free plan limits.

Diagnostics

Firebase Crashlytics collects anonymous crash reports (device model, Android version, app version, stack trace). They never contain client names or appointment content and can be turned off in Settings → Data & privacy.

Android permissions used

  • INTERNET — push reminders, usage counters, subscriptions
  • READ_CONTACTS — optional contact picker; the list is never uploaded
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — appointment and warranty reminders
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED — restore scheduled local notifications after a restart

The app does not declare SEND_SMS, CALL_PHONE or SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM. Calls and SMS are opened through the system dialer and SMS app, which need no runtime permission.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Manage appointments, clients and their history
  • Send push reminders for upcoming appointments
  • Prepare SMS confirmations and payment reminders for you to send
  • Issue estimates and invoices and create backups
  • Enforce free-plan limits
  • Diagnose crashes and fix bugs

3. Data Storage & Security

Local storage

Appointment data, clients, notes, images and documents are stored in a SQLite (Drift) database on your device. The app does not upload this data except for the minimal reminder copy described above.

Security

  • Data is accessible only to the app and the operating system
  • Network operations use HTTPS
  • Crash reports contain no client details

Important: You are responsible for device security (screen lock, encryption, etc.). We are not liable for data loss caused by unauthorized device access.

4. Third-Party Services

Google Firebase

Firestore stores the minimal reminder copy. Cloud Messaging delivers push notifications. Crashlytics collects anonymous diagnostics.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Cloudflare

Processes the pseudonymous device hash and monthly counters used for free-plan limits.

Vercel

Runs the scheduling service that reads the reminder copies and sends push notifications through Firebase.

Adapty and Google Play

Handle subscription purchases. We never see or store your card details.

Adapty Privacy Policy: https://adapty.io/privacy

5. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. Data is processed only by the providers above, to run the app, and when required by law.

6. Data Retention

  • Local data: until you delete it or uninstall the app
  • Firestore reminder copies: deleted with the appointment, the client, or all data
  • Backups: until you delete them or use "Delete all data"
  • Crashlytics reports: according to Firebase policy (default 90 days)

7. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

  • Access: all data is visible in the app
  • Rectification: edit any record in the app
  • Erasure: delete a client, or use Settings → Data & privacy → Delete all data to remove local data, images, backups and cloud copies
  • Portability: export a JSON backup from Settings
  • Objection/restriction: turn off crash reports, or stop using the app
